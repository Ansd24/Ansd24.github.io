<p class="lead text-muted mb-4">Below are the key tasks completed as part of this project.</p>

<hr class="mb-5">

<!-- Task 1 -->
<div class="card shadow-sm mb-4">
  <div class="card-body p-4">
    <h4 class="mb-3" style="border-left: 4px solid #0d6efd; padding-left: 12px;">Security Posture Assessment</h4>
    <p style="line-height: 1.9; text-align: justify;">Reviewed the security infrastructure of a multinational company to evaluate its overall security posture. Critical weaknesses were identified across networks, endpoints, and data management practices. Security improvements were prioritised using risk-based decision making, with each finding evaluated for its business impact and compliance implications across relevant regulatory frameworks.</p>
    <div class="mt-3">
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Task 2 -->
<div class="card shadow-sm mb-4">
  <div class="card-body p-4">
    <h4 class="mb-3" style="border-left: 4px solid #0d6efd; padding-left: 12px;">Security Improvement Strategy</h4>
    <p style="line-height: 1.9; text-align: justify;">Developed a set of targeted recommendations to strengthen the organisation's security controls, paired with implementation roadmaps and realistic delivery schedules. Each recommendation was evaluated against the 5Cs framework — Change, Compliance, Cost, Continuity, and Coverage — to ensure proposals were operationally viable and balanced security gains against business constraints.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Task 3 -->
<div class="card shadow-sm mb-4">
  <div class="card-body p-4">
    <h4 class="mb-3" style="border-left: 4px solid #0d6efd; padding-left: 12px;">Cyber Threat Analysis</h4>
    <p style="line-height: 1.9; text-align: justify;">Researched current cybersecurity threats relevant to enterprise organisations and performed qualitative risk assessments to evaluate their likelihood and potential impact. The business consequences of each attack scenario were examined in depth, and appropriate mitigation strategies and security controls were proposed to reduce exposure and improve organisational resilience.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Task 4 -->
<div class="card shadow-sm mb-4">
  <div class="card-body p-4">
    <h4 class="mb-3" style="border-left: 4px solid #0d6efd; padding-left: 12px;">PCI DSS Compliance Review</h4>
    <p style="line-height: 1.9; text-align: justify;">Analysed the organisation's adherence to PCI DSS requirements, identifying compliance gaps and areas of non-conformance. Security and governance improvements were recommended to close identified gaps, with technical controls mapped to specific PCI DSS requirements and broader industry standards to demonstrate how each recommendation supports a compliant and auditable environment.</p>
  </div>
</div>
